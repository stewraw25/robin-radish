Aphids without a spray

By Robin & Radish · 17 November 2026

What to do about aphids on UK beans, chillies and soft growth, using your fingers, water and the insects that eat them.

Aphids are the green, black or grey clusters on soft tips. On this site you will meet them on broad beans, French beans, runner beans and chillies. They also turn up on roses and on the tips of young brassicas. A small colony is normal. A shoot completely coated in them is the point where the plant suffers.

They suck sap. The plant grows slowly, the leaves curl, and a sticky residue appears, which then goes black with sooty mould. Ants often farm them. The ants are a clue, not the thing to poison.

Start with the least damage

Squash a small colony with your fingers, or wipe it off. On broad beans, pinching out the soft top once the plants have set a good crop of pods removes the aphids’ favourite bit and helps the pods fill. Do not pinch a plant that has only just started flowering if you still want it to grow on.

A sharp spray of water from a hose or a syringe knocks a lot of them off a sturdy plant. It is useless on a seedling you will break. Do it in the morning so the leaves dry.

After that, wait. Hoverfly larvae, ladybirds and lacewings eat aphids. A garden with flowers through the summer, and no routine insecticide, usually has those predators. Spraying the aphids also sprays the insects that were about to eat them. That is the main reason this page does not send you to a bottle.

If a chilli in a pot is overwhelmed, take it outside and wash the colony off, then look again in a few days. A heavy infestation on a windowsill, with no predators in the room, will not clear itself.

What else is going on

Curled leaves can be aphids, or they can be virus, or just heat. Uncurl a leaf. If it is full of insects, you have your answer. If it is empty and the whole plant is yellow and stunted, pinching will not fix it.

Black bean aphid on broad beans is the common one. It does not mean you did something wrong. It means the beans are growing. See also slugs, which are a different animal and need a different response. Do not put slug pellets on an aphid problem.

The sowing months for the beans are on the sowing calendar. An early sowing of broad beans often sets pods before the aphids peak. That is a useful side effect, not a guarantee.

Common questions

Should I use a soap spray or washing-up liquid?

A proper insecticidal soap, used as the label says, knocks aphids down. Washing-up liquid is not the same product, and a strong mix can scorch leaves. This site would still rather you squash or wash them off first. If you use a soap spray, keep it off the bird bath and off open flowers where pollinators are working.

Do aphids spread to the bird feeders?

No. The insects on your beans are not a problem for the feeder. Keep the two jobs separate. Do not hang a feeder in a cloud of spray, and do not stop feeding birds because the broad beans have blackfly.
