Garden birds

  • Attracting robins

    Food, cover and a nest box that suit robins in a UK garden, and how to offer food in a hanging feeder rather than on a bird table.

  • Bird feeder buying guide

    How to choose a hanging feeder, a nyjer feeder or a suet cage for a UK garden, following the RSPB's preference for hanging feeders over bird tables. No prices or rankings.

  • Bird food for winter

    From 1 November to 30 April the RSPB says you can offer seeds and peanuts as well as mealworms and suet, in hanging feeders that stay clean and dry. Bread is not on that list.

  • Cleaning bird feeders

    Clean and move bird feeders every week, change the water every day, and avoid flat feeders such as bird tables, following the RSPB's current hygiene advice.

  • Feeding birds in spring and summer

    The RSPB's current advice is to pause seeds and peanuts from 1 May to 31 October. Small amounts of mealworms, fat balls or suet can still go out, in hanging feeders that you clean weekly.

    Which UK garden birds use a hole box, which need an open front, and where to put a box so it has a chance of being used.

  • Water for garden birds
