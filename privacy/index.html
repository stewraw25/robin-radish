Privacy

17 November 2026

Robin & Radish is a small UK garden guide. This page describes this version of the site. It is not a shop account and it does not ask you to sign in.

What this site stores

There is no account, no newsletter and no analytics script in this version. The sowing planner and the bird food quiz run in your browser. Your answers are not sent to a server. The email box on the sowing calendar page is a placeholder. It is switched off, and it does not store or send an address.

The host may keep ordinary server logs, such as the address of a page request. That is the host's processing, not a mailing list.

Shop links

If you follow a link to Thompson & Morgan, Crocus, Haith's or CJ Wildlife, you leave this site. The shop, and the affiliate network it uses (Awin or Webgains), may set their own cookies and see that the visit came from here when an affiliate ID is switched on. Their privacy notices apply from that point.
