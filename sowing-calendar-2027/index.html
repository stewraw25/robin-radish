UK sowing calendar 2027

Three free PDFs. The full calendar is A3 and 14 pages. The plotting wallchart is one A3 sheet. March is also an A4 page for a home printer. Crop months are the same list as the sowing planner. South is southern England, the Midlands and much of Wales. North is northern England and Scotland, where that list differs. Bank holidays on the sheet are England and Wales. In Ireland, read sowing times in Ireland before treating these months as local.

  • Full A3 calendar14 pages. Free. 18.1 MB.
  • A3 plotting wallchart1 page. Free. 2.6 MB.
  • A4 March sample1 page. Free. 2.2 MB.

Jobs are written for the whole month. The list does not pin a sowing to one day, or to early, mid or late. The plates are drawings made for this calendar. They are not photographs.

What to sow by month, hardening off and sowing little and often use the same months.
