Birds eat fruit buds, ripening berries and brassica leaves. [Bullfinches](/birds/bullfinch) take buds in spring. [Blackbirds](/birds/blackbird) and [song thrushes](/birds/song-thrush) take berries. [Wood pigeons](/birds/wood-pigeon) strip [kale](/grow/kale) and [purple sprouting broccoli](/grow/purple-sprouting-broccoli). A net is the straightforward answer. A badly hung net is also how birds get injured.

This page is about the net, not about stopping feeding. A garden can have a feeder and a netted row. They are different jobs.

## Hang it so nothing can walk in or get tangled

Use a net with a mesh small enough that a bird cannot push its head through and then stick. A wide mesh is worse than no net for that reason.

Keep the net off the crop. A fruit cage, canes with jam jars on top, or a frame of poles, holds the mesh above the branches and the leaves. A net draped on the bush snags legs and beaks.

Peg or bury the bottom edge. Pigeons walk in under a net that stops at knee height. Leave no gap wide enough for a blackbird, because a bird that gets in cannot always find the way out.

Check the net when you walk past. A trapped bird needs the mesh cut, not pulled. Pulling tightens it. Look in the morning. A bird caught overnight is in a bad way by lunchtime.

Take the net off when the crop is finished, or when the buds have opened and you are past the bullfinch stage. A net left on all year sags, catches hedgehogs at the base, and is still a trap in the nesting season.

## What you are protecting

Buds: net a small fruit tree or bush while the buds are swelling if bullfinches are stripping it. Remove it once the flowers are open if you can do without it, so pollinators are not kept out. If you have to leave it, use a mesh insects can pass and lift it to pick.

Berries: net as the fruit colours, not in winter. Blackbirds will find an uncovered bush the day the fruit is soft.

Brassicas: a net or a cage from planting until you are picking. Peg it down. The [wood pigeon](/birds/wood-pigeon) page says the same.

Do not use a fine insect mesh as your only bird net if it is lying on the leaves. It excludes the carrot fly, which is a different job, described in [carrot fly](/guides/carrot-fly). For birds you need a frame and a check.

## Netting

You can [search Amazon for fruit netting](https://www.amazon.co.uk/s?k=fruit+netting+garden). That is a search, not a particular net. Buy a mesh you can drape over a frame and peg, and check it can be lifted to pick the fruit. A net lying on the crop is the one that traps birds.

## Common questions

### Is it legal to net fruit?

Yes, to protect a crop you are growing. It is not a licence to leave birds tangled. Check the net, use a frame, and do not set a net where you will forget it. This is not legal advice. It is the practical standard that keeps the crop and the birds apart.

### Will a scarecrow or a CD do it?

Sometimes, for a few days. Birds get used to them. A net that is actually closed is the method that still works in week three. Use the scarecrow if you like the look of it. Do not rely on it for the kale.
