A feeder is a way to keep food dry and off the ground, or to hold it where a particular bird can land. It is not a requirement. Birds use a garden with no feeder at all. This page describes four ordinary types and which birds use them. It is not a ranking, it has no prices, and it does not score any brand.

What to put in them is in [bird food for winter](/guides/bird-food-for-winter). Cleaning is in [cleaning bird feeders](/guides/feeder-hygiene). Water, including ice, is in [water for garden birds](/guides/water-for-garden-birds). If you would rather start from the birds you see, use the [bird food quiz](/quiz).

## Four types

**A hanging seed feeder**, a tube or a cage with perches, is the usual first feeder. [Blue tits](/birds/blue-tit), [great tits](/birds/great-tit) and [goldfinches](/birds/goldfinch) use it for sunflower hearts. Choose one you can take apart. The base is where old seed sits, and a feeder you cannot open is a feeder you will not clean.

**A nyjer feeder** has very small holes or a fine mesh. Goldfinches and [siskins](/birds/siskin) take nyjer from it. In a normal tube, nyjer pours through and is wasted. Most other birds leave nyjer alone, which is useful if pigeons clear everything else. Buy this when those birds are already visiting, not as the only feeder in a garden that has none.

**A ground tray or bird table** suits birds that do not cling. [Robins](/birds/robin) and [blackbirds](/birds/blackbird) take mealworms and scraps of suet from a tray. [Redwings](/birds/redwing) and [fieldfares](/birds/fieldfare) use apples on the lawn more than any feeder. Put the tray where a bird can see around it, with a shrub a short dash away, and not in long grass beside a fence a cat uses. A low tray needs cleaning as often as a tube, because it collects rain.

**A suet cage** holds a block, pellets or fat balls. Tits, [starlings](/birds/starling) and [woodpeckers](/birds/great-spotted-woodpecker) use it in cold weather. [Long-tailed tits](/birds/long-tailed-tit) often come to suet as a flock. If a mild spell makes the fat soft enough to smear, take it down. Fat on feathers does not help the bird. A cage you can open is easier to wash than a net bag you throw away full of slime.

You do not need all four. Sunflower hearts in a hanging feeder, plus a tray if a robin is the bird you want to see, covers a lot of ordinary gardens. Add nyjer or suet when you have a reason.

## Where it hangs

High enough that a cat cannot spring from a fence onto it, and near enough that you will notice when the food is damp. A pole with a baffle slows many squirrels. It does not stop all of them. A cage around the seed lets small birds in and keeps [wood pigeons](/birds/wood-pigeon) and [collared doves](/birds/collared-dove) out. Siting the feeder away from a jumping-off branch helps more than a second packet of seed.

Move it a short distance now and then so droppings are not always on the same patch. Sweep spilled seed. A carpet of old food attracts rats.

## What not to put in them

Do not use a feeder for salted, dry-roasted or flavoured nuts, desiccated coconut, milk, or cooked food. Whole peanuts belong in a rigid mesh feeder so birds chip pieces off, rather than loose where a bird can carry a whole nut to a nest. Bread is a poor staple in any feeder.

A heater for a bird bath is optional to the point of being unnecessary. Breaking the ice in the morning is the method on this site. Do not add salt or antifreeze to water.

## Common questions

### How many feeders does a small garden need?

One, kept clean, is enough to start. A second is worth adding when you want a different food, such as nyjer for goldfinches or a tray for robins. Four feeders in a small yard mostly create more cleaning.

### Will any feeder do for every bird?

No. Robins rarely use a hanging tube as their first choice. Goldfinches waste nyjer in a feeder with large ports. Pigeons will sit on an open table and finish mixed seed. Match the feeder to the bird, or accept that some species will feed elsewhere in the garden.

### Does the price of a feeder tell me if it is safe for birds?

This site does not use price, and it has no scores. Check whether you can clean the feeder, whether rain pools in the base, and whether the food stays dry. A feeder left full of wet seed is the problem, whatever it cost.
