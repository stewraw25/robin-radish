A feeder is a way to keep food dry and off the ground, or to hold it where a particular bird can land. It is not a requirement. Birds use a garden with no feeder at all. This page describes ordinary hanging types and which birds use them. It is not a ranking, it has no prices, and it does not score any brand. The RSPB's current advice is to choose hanging feeders over flat feeders such as bird tables, window feeders and trays.

What to put in them is in [bird food for winter](/guides/bird-food-for-winter). Cleaning is in [cleaning bird feeders](/guides/feeder-hygiene). Water, including ice, is in [water for garden birds](/guides/water-for-garden-birds). If you would rather start from the birds you see, use the [bird food quiz](/quiz).

## Ordinary types

**A hanging seed feeder**, a tube or a cage with perches, is the usual first feeder. [Blue tits](/birds/blue-tit), [great tits](/birds/great-tit) and [goldfinches](/birds/goldfinch) use it for sunflower hearts. Choose one you can take apart. The base is where old seed sits, and a feeder you cannot open is a feeder you will not clean.

**A nyjer feeder** has very small holes or a fine mesh. Goldfinches and [siskins](/birds/siskin) take nyjer from it. In a normal tube, nyjer pours through and is wasted. Most other birds leave nyjer alone, which is useful if pigeons clear everything else. Nyjer is a seed, so it belongs in the RSPB's 1 November to 30 April window. Buy this when those birds are already visiting, not as the only feeder in a garden that has none.

**A suet cage** holds a block, pellets or fat balls. Tits, [starlings](/birds/starling) and [woodpeckers](/birds/great-spotted-woodpecker) use it in cold weather, and it is also a useful hanging offer for [robins](/birds/robin) if you put out suet or mealworms. [Long-tailed tits](/birds/long-tailed-tit) often come to suet as a flock. If a mild spell makes the fat soft enough to smear, take it down. Fat on feathers does not help the bird. A cage you can open is easier to wash than a net bag you throw away full of slime.

The RSPB advises against bird tables, window feeders and other flat trays, because contaminated food collects on them and the risk of disease is higher. [Robins](/birds/robin) and [blackbirds](/birds/blackbird) still take mealworms and suet readily from a hanging feeder. [Redwings](/birds/redwing) and [fieldfares](/birds/fieldfare) use apples on the lawn more than any feeder. If you already have a tray, keep the amount small, keep it dry, clean it every week and move it every week, or retire it.

You do not need every type. Sunflower hearts in a hanging feeder from 1 November to 30 April, plus a hanging suet or mealworm feeder if a robin is the bird you want to see, covers a lot of ordinary gardens. Add nyjer in that same winter-and-spring window when goldfinches give you a reason.

## Where it hangs

High enough that a cat cannot spring from a fence onto it, and near enough that you will notice when the food is damp. A pole with a baffle slows many squirrels. It does not stop all of them. A cage around the seed lets small birds in and keeps [wood pigeons](/birds/wood-pigeon) and [collared doves](/birds/collared-dove) out. Siting the feeder away from a jumping-off branch helps more than a second packet of seed.

Move it to a different spot every week so droppings are not always on the same patch. Sweep spilled seed. A carpet of old food attracts rats.

## What not to put in them

Do not use a feeder for salted, dry-roasted or flavoured nuts, desiccated coconut, milk, or cooked food. Whole peanuts belong in a rigid mesh hanging feeder, and only from 1 November to 30 April, so birds chip pieces off. Bread is not one of the foods the RSPB currently lists, and it should not be used as a staple.

A heater for a bird bath is optional to the point of being unnecessary. Breaking the ice in the morning is the method on this site. Do not add salt or antifreeze to water.

## Common questions

### How many feeders does a small garden need?

One, kept clean, is enough to start. A second is worth adding when you want a different food, such as nyjer for goldfinches or a hanging suet feeder for robins. Four feeders in a small yard mostly create more cleaning.

### Will any feeder do for every bird?

No. Goldfinches waste nyjer in a feeder with large ports. Pigeons will finish mixed seed if they can sit on an open tray, which is one reason the RSPB prefers hanging feeders. Robins often take mealworms or suet from a hanging feeder even though they also feed on the ground. Match the feeder to the bird, or accept that some species will feed elsewhere in the garden.

### Does the price of a feeder tell me if it is safe for birds?

This site does not use price, and it has no scores. Check whether you can clean the feeder, whether rain pools in the base, and whether the food stays dry. A feeder left full of wet seed is the problem, whatever it cost.
