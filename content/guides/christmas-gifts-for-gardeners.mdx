A useful present for a UK gardener is usually something they will use in winter or in early spring: a tool that can be sharpened, a roll of fleece, a tray for sowing, or food for the birds already visiting the garden. This page is a short list of examples, not a ranking and not a test. There are no scores, prices or stock claims. Listings change, so read the product page for the size and whether it is in stock.

## Tools

[Felco 2 bypass secateurs](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B00G2H2N8M) are a standard pair for cutting live stems. Bypass blades pass each other, which suits green wood better than an anvil type that crushes. Spare parts are sold for this model, so a worn spring or blade can be replaced rather than throwing the tool away. They will not cut thick, woody branches; that is a job for a lopper or a saw.

For higher or thicker stems, [Spear & Jackson Razorsharp telescopic bypass pruners](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0CTKL5VN5) have handles that extend. Check the product page for the closed and extended length, and whether it suits the person you are buying for. Winter is also the usual time to [prune fruit bushes](/guides/winter-pruning-fruit-bushes), so a gift of this kind can be used before spring growth starts.

## Sowing and frost

A [Garland 24-cell seed starter set](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B083V61V4B) is a tray, a 24-cell insert and a propagator lid. That is enough for a windowsill sowing of lettuce, chilli or tomato seed later in winter, without needing a greenhouse. The [sowing under cover](/guides/sowing-under-cover) guide covers how to use a tray. [How to chit seed potatoes](/guides/chitting-potatoes) is a separate January job and does not need a propagator.

[Kingfisher horticultural fleece](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B005UFMALC) is a sheet you can cut to cover a row or wrap a shrub. [Smart Garden plant warming fleece covers](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B07N8ZR838) are bags with a drawstring, made to go over a pot or a small plant. Fleece takes the edge off a light frost. It is not a substitute for bringing a tender pot indoors, and a cover left sealed in damp weather can sit wet. The longer note is [first frost dates and protecting plants](/guides/first-frost-dates).

## For the birds

[Extra Select sunflower hearts](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B007QD0J9Q) are hulled sunflower seed, used in a hanging feeder. They do not leave husks on the ground. A large bag is a useful present if the household already feeds birds, and a smaller size exists on the same listing if a 12.75 kg sack is too much to store. Keep the food dry and the feeder clean; see [bird food for winter](/guides/bird-food-for-winter) and [feeder hygiene](/guides/feeder-hygiene).

A [Walter Harrison's nest box with interchangeable entrances](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B06ZYLTC9X) lets you change the hole size, which matters because different birds use different holes. A 25 mm hole suits a blue tit; a larger hole lets bigger birds in. The box still may not be used. Put it up in winter, on a quiet side of the garden, and leave it alone in the breeding season. The [nest box guide](/guides/nest-boxes) has the hole sizes.

[Haith's](https://haiths.com/) and [CJ Wildlife](https://www.birdfood.co.uk/) also sell bird food and feeders if you would rather buy from a specialist shop.

## What not to wrap

Do not give kitchen garlic or supermarket potatoes as seed. Use bulbs and tubers sold for planting, as the [garlic](/grow/garlic) and [early potatoes](/grow/early-potatoes) pages explain. Do not give a nest box that cannot be opened to clean, or a feeder that cannot be taken apart. Those details are on the product page, not on this list.

This is not a complete shop, and it is not a claim that these are the only, or the most popular, versions. If a listing has gone, the same kind of item from another maker will do the same job.
