Birds need water to drink and to bathe. A feeder does not replace it. In a UK garden the two times it matters most are a hard freeze, when every puddle is ice, and a dry spell, when the bird bath is the only water in the yard.

Food notes are separate. See [bird food for winter](/guides/bird-food-for-winter) and the [bird food quiz](/quiz). Species pages, including [robin](/birds/robin) and [blackbird](/birds/blackbird), say what they eat. This page is only the water.

## A dish that works

Use a shallow dish. A plant saucer or a low bowl is enough. Two or three centimetres of water lets a small bird stand. A stone in the middle gives a wren or a robin a place to perch. A deep bucket is a risk, not a bath.

Put it where the bird can see around it, with a shrub a short dash away, and not tucked against a fence a cat can hide behind. The same compromise as a ground tray. If the cat sits under it, move the dish.

Change the water when it is dirty. Bathing birds leave droppings in it. In warm weather that happens quickly. The [cleaning guide](/guides/feeder-hygiene) covers the wash. Rinse the dish. You do not need a scented cleaner.

## Frost and hot weather

In a freeze, break the ice in the morning and again later if you are there. Pour a little warm water on the ice if you need to. Do not add salt, antifreeze or glycerine. Salt and antifreeze harm birds. Glycerine is sometimes suggested and is not worth the experiment. Fresh water, refreshed, is the method.

A dish left out overnight will freeze again. That is expected. Top it up in the morning, when birds are looking.

In summer, a dry week matters as much as January. Check the dish when you water the pots. Site it out of the full afternoon sun if the water is turning green every day, and empty it rather than topping up slime.

Do not tip the dirty water onto the vegetable bed if you can put it on a path. It is not a feed.

## A dish or a bath

A plant saucer or a shallow bowl is enough. If you want a purpose-made dish, you can [search Amazon for a bird bath](https://www.amazon.co.uk/s?k=bird+bath). That is a search, not a particular model. What matters is that you can empty it, scrub it, and keep the water shallow.

## Common questions

### Should I buy a heater for the bird bath?

You do not need one. Breaking the ice and refilling is enough for a garden dish. A heater is another thing to keep out of the water when it fails. The morning visit is the useful habit.

### Will a pond do instead?

A pond with a shallow edge will. A steep-sided barrel will not, because a small bird cannot climb out. If the only water is deep, put the saucer out as well.

### Do birds bathe in winter?

Yes. Bathing keeps feathers working, including in cold weather. That is why the water should be clean and free of anything you would not drink yourself. Offer it, and let the bird decide.
