You do not have to feed birds in spring and summer. Insects, buds and seed are easier to find than they were in January. If you stop, the birds that live in the garden do not vanish. If you carry on, keep the amounts small and the feeder clean. Warm, wet weather spoils food faster than a frost does.

Winter foods are listed in [bird food for winter](/guides/bird-food-for-winter). The short chooser is the [quiz](/quiz). Cleaning, and what to do if birds look unwell, is [cleaning bird feeders](/guides/feeder-hygiene). This page is the change of season, and the foods to leave out.

## What changes in the breeding season

[Blue tits](/birds/blue-tit) and [great tits](/birds/great-tit) hunt caterpillars once the leaves are out. A feeder is a supplement, not the diet they feed their young. [Robins](/birds/robin) still take mealworms from a tray. Soak dried ones. Parents also need the insects they find in the hedge. Do not expect a tray to replace that.

Whole peanuts stay in a rigid mesh feeder. A parent can carry a loose whole nut to a nest. In a mesh feeder the bird has to chip pieces off. That rule matters more in spring than in January.

Suet and fat balls go soft in a warm spell. If the fat smears, take it down. Soft fat on feathers is a problem. A mild week in April is enough. Put suet out again when the weather is cold, not on a hot afternoon.

Keep a shallow dish of water. A dry May matters as much as a freeze. [Water for garden birds](/guides/water-for-garden-birds) covers the dish. Change it when it is dirty. Bathing birds use it, and so do nestlings' parents.

## What not to put out

Do not put out salted, dry-roasted or flavoured nuts, mouldy or damp seed, milk, desiccated coconut, or cooked food seasoned the way you would eat it. Cooking fat that has held salt is not a suet cake.

Bread is a poor staple. A few stale crumbs are not a meal, and they go sour if they sit. They should not be the only thing on the table.

Loose whole peanuts, on a table or the ground, are the wrong offer in nesting season. Use the mesh feeder or do not put them out.

Windfall apples on the ground are still useful for [blackbirds](/birds/blackbird). That is fruit, not scraps from a plate. Do not add leftovers.

If seed is clumped or smells stale, throw it away. Do not mix it back into the bag. In summer, put out a day of food rather than a feeder that sits full for a week.

## If you go away

Empty the feeder and the bath before you leave, or ask someone to do the same small top-up you do. A feeder of rotting seed for a fortnight is worse than no feeder. Birds used the garden before the holiday. They will use it when you are back, provided you start again with clean equipment.

## Common questions

### Should I stop feeding in April?

You can. If you keep going, switch the habit: less food, no soft suet, peanuts only in a mesh, and a clean feeder. Stopping is a reasonable choice. Stopping and leaving old seed in the tube is not.

### Can I feed mealworms while birds are nesting?

Yes, on a tray, as a supplement. Soak dried mealworms. The chicks of tits and robins still need the insects their parents catch. Mealworms are not a complete replacement for that.

### What if finches at the feeder look fluffed and slow?

Take the feeders and the bath down, clean them, clear spilled seed, and pause. The [cleaning guide](/guides/feeder-hygiene) sets out the steps. That is general garden practice, not a diagnosis, and it applies in summer as well as winter.
