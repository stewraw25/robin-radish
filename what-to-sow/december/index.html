UK sowing calendar

What to sow in December

December is the quietest sowing month. Garlic is the planting if you missed autumn and the soil is not frozen. There is nothing useful to sow outdoors in this list. Harvest leeks, parsnips, sprouts and kale as you need them, and check stored potatoes and onions for rot. Leave the digging if the ground is wet. A windowsill pot of pea shoots is a kitchen job, not a garden row.

South means southern England, the Midlands and much of Wales. North means northern England and Scotland. Frost, soil and a cold spring move the dates. These are ranges, not a forecast for one garden.

In the south, 6 crops have a sowing, planting or harvest job this month. In the north, 5. The lists are the same crops as the sowing planner.

Garden birds this month

Short days. Blue tits, coal tits and great spotted woodpeckers use suet and sunflower hearts. Starlings finish suet quickly. Robins and blackbirds need a tray, mealworms and fruit. Fieldfares and redwings take apples on the lawn. A blackcap may take fruit and suet from the table. Collared doves pick up spilled seed. Break ice on a shallow dish, and fill feeders in the morning.

  • RobinA small, upright bird with an orange-red breast that feeds on the ground.
  • BlackbirdA familiar thrush. Males are black with a yellow beak. Females are brown.
  • Blue titA tiny, acrobatic tit with a blue cap and yellow underparts.
  • Coal titA small, grey-buff tit with a white patch on the back of the neck.
  • StarlingA noisy, short-tailed bird that looks black, and spotted in winter.
  • FieldfareA large, noisy winter thrush with a grey head, chestnut back and a dark tail.
  • RedwingA small thrush with a cream stripe over the eye and a rusty patch under the wing, mostly seen in winter.
  • BlackcapA grey warbler. The male has a black cap, the female a brown one. Some spend winter in gardens.
  • Collared doveA slim, pale dove with a thin black half-collar, common in gardens all year.

Common questions

Is there anything to sow outdoors in December?

Not in this list. Garlic can still be planted if the soil is workable. Everything else waits. Forcing a row of peas into frozen ground wastes the seed.

Which vegetables stand through December?

Leeks, parsnips, Brussels sprouts and kale are the ones in this list with a December harvest in both regions. Cut or lift what you will eat and leave the rest until you can work the bed without smearing the soil.

What should a December feeder hold?

Sunflower hearts and suet as the base. Nyjer if goldfinches are about. Mealworms on a tray for robins. Fresh water, with the ice broken. Take suet down if a mild spell makes it soft enough to smear on feathers.
