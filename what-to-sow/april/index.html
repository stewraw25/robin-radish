UK sowing calendar

What to sow in April

April is a full sowing month. Hardy crops go outdoors in both halves of the country when the soil is fit. Tender plants are still raised under cover. A late frost is still normal, particularly in the north and on high ground. Early potatoes go in. Maincrop potatoes go in this month too.

South means southern England, the Midlands and much of Wales. North means northern England and Scotland. Frost, soil and a cold spring move the dates. These are ranges, not a forecast for one garden.

In the south, 35 crops have a sowing, planting or harvest job this month. In the north, 35. The lists are the same crops as the sowing planner.

Garden birds this month

Nesting is underway for some tits, robins and blackbirds, and others are still building. Wrens are singing from the hedge. Keep feeding if you want to, in small fresh amounts, and do not cut hedges. Mealworms are useful for robins and blackbirds. Water still matters.

  • RobinA small, upright bird with an orange-red breast that feeds on the ground.
  • Blue titA tiny, acrobatic tit with a blue cap and yellow underparts.
  • Great titThe largest common tit, with a black head, white cheeks and a stripe down the belly.
  • WrenA tiny brown bird with a cocked tail and a very loud song.
  • House sparrowA stocky, sociable brown bird with a thick beak, often in a noisy group.
  • BlackbirdA familiar thrush. Males are black with a yellow beak. Females are brown.
  • Song thrushA spotted thrush, smaller than a blackbird, that hunts on the lawn and smashes snail shells.
  • BullfinchA plump finch with a black cap and a short, heavy bill. The male has a rose-pink breast.

Common questions

Can tomatoes go outside in April?

Not in this list. Planting out is May in the south and June in the north, after the nights have warmed. A greenhouse is a different place from the open bed.

Is April too late to sow leeks?

No. Outdoor sowing of leeks runs through March and April in both regions. They are planted into their final spacing in early summer.

Should bird food stop in April?

It does not have to. Keep it fresh and keep the feeder clean, because birds are nesting or about to. A huge pile of damp seed is the thing to stop, not a modest clean offering.
