UK sowing calendar

What to sow in February

February is still winter, with longer days. In the south, broad beans and peas can be started in pots under cover, and onion seed wants a bright windowsill. Outdoor sowing waits until the soil is workable. In the north, keep most of this under cover, or wait for March. Chit early potatoes somewhere light and frost-free.

South means southern England, the Midlands and much of Wales. North means northern England and Scotland. Frost, soil and a cold spring move the dates. These are ranges, not a forecast for one garden.

In the south, 19 crops have a sowing, planting or harvest job this month. In the north, 13. The lists are the same crops as the sowing planner.

Garden birds this month

Feeders are still busy. Coal tits snatch a seed and hide it. Long-tailed tits arrive as a party on the suet. Chaffinches work the ground underneath. Dunnocks shuffle there too. Wrens stay in the hedge and rarely use the feeder. This is a sound month to put nest boxes up, before most birds are choosing sites.

  • Coal titA small, grey-buff tit with a white patch on the back of the neck.
  • Long-tailed titA tiny pink, black and white bird with a tail longer than its body.
  • ChaffinchA sparrow-sized finch. Males are pink and blue-grey, with white wing bars.
  • DunnockA quiet, sparrow-like bird with a fine bill that shuffles under the feeder.
  • WrenA tiny brown bird with a cocked tail and a very loud song.
  • RobinA small, upright bird with an orange-red breast that feeds on the ground.
  • BullfinchA plump finch with a black cap and a short, heavy bill. The male has a rose-pink breast.

Common questions

Can I sow carrots outdoors in February?

Not in this list. The soil is too cold for a reliable germination. Outdoor carrot sowing here starts in March in the south and April in the north.

Should early potatoes be chitting in February?

Yes, if you have the tubers. Stand them in a light, frost-free place so the shoots stay short and green. Planting is March or April, later in the north. Maincrops are planted in April and do not need a long chit.

Are garden birds nesting in February?

A few robins and blackbirds look at sites late in winter. Most nesting is later. Putting a box up now means it is in place before they choose. Do not open a box that a bird is already using.
