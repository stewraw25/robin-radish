UK sowing calendar

What to sow in October

October is garlic month, in the north and the south, as long as the soil is workable. In the south, broad beans, hardy peas and overwintering onion sets can go in too. Lift maincrop potatoes if they are still in the ground. Parsnips can stay put. Mark rows of roots before the tops disappear.

South means southern England, the Midlands and much of Wales. North means northern England and Scotland. Frost, soil and a cold spring move the dates. These are ranges, not a forecast for one garden.

In the south, 31 crops have a sowing, planting or harvest job this month. In the north, 25. The lists are the same crops as the sowing planner.

Garden birds this month

Long-tailed tits return to suet in small flocks. Coal tits cache seed. Goldfinches use nyjer. Starlings take suet. Robins and blackbirds take mealworms and apples. Winter thrushes may start to appear on fruit. Do not put out salted or cooked food.

  • RobinA small, upright bird with an orange-red breast that feeds on the ground.
  • BlackbirdA familiar thrush. Males are black with a yellow beak. Females are brown.
  • Long-tailed titA tiny pink, black and white bird with a tail longer than its body.
  • Coal titA small, grey-buff tit with a white patch on the back of the neck.
  • GoldfinchA small finch with a red, white and black face and a yellow bar on the wing.
  • StarlingA noisy, short-tailed bird that looks black, and spotted in winter.
  • SiskinA small, streaky yellow-green finch that hangs on nyjer and alder cones, often in winter.
  • NuthatchA blue-grey bird with a black eye-stripe that runs head-first down a tree trunk.

Common questions

What is planted in October?

Garlic in both regions. In the south, also broad beans, hardy peas and overwintering onion sets. In the north, broad beans can be sown outdoors. Hardy peas and overwintering sets are left off the northern list here.

Can parsnips stay in the ground?

Yes. Harvest runs from October through winter in both regions. Mark the row, because the foliage dies back and the roots are then invisible.

What bird food suits October?

Sunflower hearts, suet and, if goldfinches visit, nyjer. Mealworms on a tray for robins and blackbirds. Windfall apples for blackbirds. Keep seed dry. October rain clumps a feeder that was filled too full.
