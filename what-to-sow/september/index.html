UK sowing calendar

What to sow in September

September is the turn toward winter. Outdoor sowing is a short list: spinach, coriander and, in the south, radishes. Lettuce can be sown under cover for a late picking. In milder southern gardens, overwintering onion sets can be planted. Finish picking tender crops before a frost blackens them. Garlic is next month if the bed is free.

South means southern England, the Midlands and much of Wales. North means northern England and Scotland. Frost, soil and a cold spring move the dates. These are ranges, not a forecast for one garden.

In the south, 30 crops have a sowing, planting or harvest job this month. In the north, 29. The lists are the same crops as the sowing planner.

Garden birds this month

Robins become obvious again and sing. Blue tits and great tits spend more time on feeders. Chaffinches and goldfinches are still on seed. Blackbirds start to take fallen apples. There is no fixed day to begin winter feeding. A clean feeder of sunflower hearts is a simple start.

  • RobinA small, upright bird with an orange-red breast that feeds on the ground.
  • Great titThe largest common tit, with a black head, white cheeks and a stripe down the belly.
  • Blue titA tiny, acrobatic tit with a blue cap and yellow underparts.
  • ChaffinchA sparrow-sized finch. Males are pink and blue-grey, with white wing bars.
  • GoldfinchA small finch with a red, white and black face and a yellow bar on the wing.
  • BlackbirdA familiar thrush. Males are black with a yellow beak. Females are brown.

Common questions

Can broad beans be sown in September?

Not in this list. Autumn sowing of broad beans is October and November in the south, and October in the north. September seed often grows too soft before winter.

What can be planted in September?

Overwintering onion sets in the south. Mint can be moved or split into a pot. Most other planting in this list waits for October, when garlic goes in.

When should winter feeding start?

When you are ready to keep the food fresh. September is when many gardens put feeders back into daily use. Sunflower hearts and clean water are enough to begin. Add suet when the weather turns cold.
