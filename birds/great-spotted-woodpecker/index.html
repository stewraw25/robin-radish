UK garden bird

Great spotted woodpecker

A black-and-white woodpecker, starling-sized, that clings upright to trunks and feeders.

The drawing is a silhouette for shape only. Colours are in the text.

How to recognise one

Bold black and white, with a red patch under the tail. The male has a red patch on the back of the head. Females lack that nape patch. Young birds have a red crown. It is about the size of a starling, with a stiff tail that it braces against bark, and a straight, chisel-shaped beak. Flight is undulating. The smaller lesser spotted woodpecker is sparrow-sized and much less often seen in gardens.

What to feed a great spotted woodpecker

Insects under bark, and in gardens suet and peanuts in a mesh feeder. They also take sunflower hearts. A suet cake hung against a trunk, or a feeder they can cling to, is the usual setup. They can dominate a feeder for a few minutes and then leave. In spring they drum on dead wood. That is a display, not them drilling the house for food.

Nesting

They excavate a hole in a tree, often a dead trunk or a soft deciduous tree, and do not use a standard nest box. If you have a dead tree that is safe to leave, it is more useful than a box. They usually raise one brood. Starlings sometimes take over a woodpecker hole.

Buying food

Straight foods such as sunflower hearts, suet and mealworms. This is not a ranking of shops or of any bag.

Common questions

What should I feed a great spotted woodpecker in the UK?

Insects under bark, and in gardens suet and peanuts in a mesh feeder. They also take sunflower hearts. A suet cake hung against a trunk, or a feeder they can cling to, is the usual setup. They can dominate a feeder for a few minutes and then leave. In spring they drum on dead wood. That is a display, not them drilling the house for food.

Something is drumming on the tree in spring. Is it feeding?

A short, rapid drum in spring is a great spotted woodpecker declaring a territory. Feeding sounds are slower taps while the bird works along a branch. Drumming on a metal vent or a television aerial is the same display.

Will a woodpecker use a nest box?

Not the boxes sold for tits. They dig their own hole in wood. Leaving a standing dead trunk, where it cannot fall on anything, is the practical help.
