UK garden bird

Greenfinch

A stocky olive-green finch with a heavy beak and yellow in the wing.

The drawing is a silhouette for shape only. Colours are in the text.

How to recognise one

Sparrow-sized or a little larger, with a forked tail and a pale, conical beak. Males are olive green with yellow on the wing and the side of the tail. Females are duller and more streaked. In dull light they can look plain grey-green. The call is a wheezy note.

What to feed a greenfinch

Sunflower hearts are the straightforward food. They also take sunflower seed in the shell and will use a table or a hanging feeder. They are less interested in nyjer than goldfinches are. Keep the feeder clean. Finches are the birds that suffer first when dirty feeders are left up.

Try the bird food quiz if you want a short list of foods for the birds you actually see. Winter food, spring and summer and water sit alongside the species notes.

Nesting

A cup of twigs, grass and moss in a dense shrub or a conifer. They often nest in loose groups. Leave evergreen cover untrimmed through spring and summer. The cleaning guide explains when to take feeders down if birds look fluffed and listless.

Buying food

Straight foods such as sunflower hearts, suet and mealworms. This is not a ranking of shops or of any bag.

Common questions

What should I feed a greenfinch in the UK?

Sunflower hearts are the straightforward food. They also take sunflower seed in the shell and will use a table or a hanging feeder. They are less interested in nyjer than goldfinches are. Keep the feeder clean. Finches are the birds that suffer first when dirty feeders are left up.

Greenfinches at the feeder look fluffed and sleepy. What should I do?

Take the feeders and the bird bath down, clean them, rake up spilled seed, and pause feeding for a couple of weeks. That is the usual step when several finches look unwell. It is general garden practice, not a diagnosis. The feeder hygiene guide sets out the cleaning.

Will greenfinches use a nyjer feeder?

Sometimes, but sunflower hearts are the food to offer first. Nyjer is aimed at goldfinches and siskins, which have finer beaks.
