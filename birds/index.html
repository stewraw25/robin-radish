Garden birds

Birds in a UK garden

24 species that turn up at feeders, on lawns, or in hedges, including winter thrushes.

  • RobinA small, upright bird with an orange-red breast that feeds on the ground.

    Photo: Francis C. Franklin, CC BY-SA 3.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • Blue titA tiny, acrobatic tit with a blue cap and yellow underparts.

    Photo: Francis C. Franklin, CC BY-SA 3.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • Great titThe largest common tit, with a black head, white cheeks and a stripe down the belly.

    Photo: Francis C. Franklin, CC BY-SA 3.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • Coal titA small, grey-buff tit with a white patch on the back of the neck.

    Photo: Marton Berntsen, CC BY-SA 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • Long-tailed titA tiny pink, black and white bird with a tail longer than its body.

    Photo: Alexis Lours, CC BY 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • BlackbirdA familiar thrush. Males are black with a yellow beak. Females are brown.

    Photo: Andreas Trepte, CC BY-SA 2.5 via Wikimedia Commons

  • House sparrowA stocky, sociable brown bird with a thick beak, often in a noisy group.

    Photo: Arnold Paul, CC BY-SA 2.5 via Wikimedia Commons

  • GoldfinchA small finch with a red, white and black face and a yellow bar on the wing.

    Photo: Francis C. Franklin, CC BY-SA 3.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • GreenfinchA stocky olive-green finch with a heavy beak and yellow in the wing.

    Photo: Francis C. Franklin, CC BY-SA 3.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • ChaffinchA sparrow-sized finch. Males are pink and blue-grey, with white wing bars.

    Photo: Andreas Trepte, CC BY-SA 2.5 via Wikimedia Commons

  • StarlingA noisy, short-tailed bird that looks black, and spotted in winter.

    Photo: Pierre Selim, CC BY-SA 3.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • WrenA tiny brown bird with a cocked tail and a very loud song.

    Photo: Alexis Lours, CC BY 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • DunnockA quiet, sparrow-like bird with a fine bill that shuffles under the feeder.

    Photo: Charles J. Sharp, CC BY-SA 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • Wood pigeonA large grey pigeon with a white neck patch and a white bar on the wing.

    Photo: Alexey V. Kurochkin, CC BY-SA 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • Great spotted woodpeckerA black-and-white woodpecker, starling-sized, that clings upright to trunks and feeders.

    Photo: Andreas Eichler, CC BY-SA 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • Song thrushA spotted thrush, smaller than a blackbird, that hunts on the lawn and smashes snail shells.

    Photo: Andreas Trepte, CC BY-SA 2.5 via Wikimedia Commons

  • NuthatchA blue-grey bird with a black eye-stripe that runs head-first down a tree trunk.

    Photo: Isiwal, CC BY-SA 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • BullfinchA plump finch with a black cap and a short, heavy bill. The male has a rose-pink breast.

    Photo: Francis C. Franklin, CC BY-SA 3.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • SiskinA small, streaky yellow-green finch that hangs on nyjer and alder cones, often in winter.

    Photo: Isiwal, CC BY-SA 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • RedwingA small thrush with a cream stripe over the eye and a rusty patch under the wing, mostly seen in winter.

    Photo: Andreas Trepte, CC BY-SA 2.5 via Wikimedia Commons

  • FieldfareA large, noisy winter thrush with a grey head, chestnut back and a dark tail.

    Photo: Frankie Fouganthin, CC BY-SA 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • BramblingA winter finch, often with chaffinches, with an orange shoulder and a white rump.

    Photo: Pierre Dalous, CC BY-SA 3.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • BlackcapA grey warbler. The male has a black cap, the female a brown one. Some spend winter in gardens.

    Photo: Charles J. Sharp, CC BY-SA 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons

  • Collared doveA slim, pale dove with a thin black half-collar, common in gardens all year.

    Photo: Alexis Lours, CC BY 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons
