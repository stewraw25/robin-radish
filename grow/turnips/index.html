When to sow turnips

Position, spacing and care

Sun or light shade, in soil that does not dry out.

About 10 cm for small roots, a little wider if you are leaving them to size up. Rows about 25 cm apart.

Sow where they will grow. They transplant badly. Water in a dry spell. Pull early sowings while they are still small. Later sowings can stay in the ground.

Common problems

  • Flea beetle: small holes in the young leaves. A cover of fine mesh from sowing helps.
  • Woody, hot roots after a dry spell.
  • Clubroot, because a turnip is a brassica even though you eat the root.
