Purple sprouting broccoli

When to sow purple sprouting broccoli

Sow purple sprouting broccoli indoors in April to June in the south, and April to May in the north, and sow them outdoors in April to June in the south, and May to June in the north, and plant in June to July. The harvest window is February to April in the south, and March to May in the north. Sown in late spring and planted out in summer, it stands through winter and sprouts the following spring. Position, spacing and care

Sun, and firm soil that will not rock the tall plants in winter.

About 60 cm between plants. They are large by autumn.

Sow in late spring and plant out in early summer so the plants are well grown before winter. Stake them if the site is exposed. Leave them standing. The spears come the following spring. Pick often, before the flowers open.

Common problems

  • Pigeons, especially in winter when other greens are scarce.
  • Cabbage white caterpillars in summer.
  • A plant that falls over in a wet, windy winter because the soil was loose.
  • Clubroot. Treat it as a brassica when you rotate.
